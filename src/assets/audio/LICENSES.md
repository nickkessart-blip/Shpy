# SHPY audio credits

All recordings included here are shipped locally. The game does not request audio from third-party servers at runtime.

| Local file | Source | Creator / collection | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `sfx/pistol-report-1.wav`, `pistol-report-2.wav`, `pistol-report-3.wav` | [The Free Firearm Sound Library](https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library), edited excerpts from P226 / Walther PPQ recordings | Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras, Matthew Nanney | CC0 1.0 |
| `sfx/pistol-tail.mp3` | The Free Firearm Sound Library, edited P226 mid-distance tail | Original recordists listed above; edit and source mapping documented by the upstream project | CC0 1.0 source recording |
| `sfx/ui-click.wav`, `sfx/ui-confirm.wav`, `sfx/reload-mechanism.wav` | [Kenney Interface Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds) | Kenney | CC0 1.0 |
| `sfx/impact.ogg` | [Kenney Impact Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds) | Kenney | CC0 1.0 |
| `sfx/explosion.ogg` | [Kenney Sci-Fi Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds) | Kenney | CC0 1.0 |
| `music/agency-theme.ogg` | [Background Music 1](https://opengameart.org/content/background-music-1) | Tozan | CC0 1.0 |

The firearm recordings are selected from the CC0 collection and edited by the upstream [hard-lines audio project](https://github.com/yegors/hard-lines). The Kenney sound files were selected from its CC0 libraries and a mirror that preserves the upstream asset names. Music is from the OpenGameArt CC0 upload by Tozan, mirrored in the source repository's audio credits.

CC0 permits use, modification, and redistribution, including in commercial games. This file records provenance even where attribution is not legally required. Verify upstream pages again before a commercial release.
